Cadencia is a private, locally operated tool for scheduling and publishing social media content. It is currently in testing. It is not an open online service: use is limited to the operator and users the operator explicitly authorizes, and there is no public registration.

Platform rules and approvals

No guarantee of publishing

Cadencia is provided in testing, without a service-level commitment. There is no guarantee that scheduled or requested actions will succeed. Providers may reject content, apply rate limits or quotas, expire or revoke access tokens, or restrict app access pending review or audit. When that happens, publishing may fail or stop.

Verify the final remote state

  • Revoking or deleting a schedule inside Cadencia does not necessarily cancel a schedule already created on the provider.
  • The final state of any post — published, scheduled, or deleted — should be verified on the provider platform itself.
  • Remote content must be deleted on the provider platform; deleting local records does not remove it.
